# FAQ

**Is there a free trial?**
Yes - 30 days, all features, no account needed. See [Activation](activation.md).

**How much does it cost?**
$49 per year, purchased on our website (all platforms).

**How many computers can I use one license on?**
Up to 3 of your own computers, in any mix of Windows, macOS and Linux.

**Can I move my license to a new computer?**
Yes - **Help → Deactivate This Device** on the old one, then activate on the new
one.

**Does it work offline?**
Yes. It verifies online when you activate and re-checks occasionally, but works
offline in between.

**Which operating systems are supported?**
Windows 10/11, macOS and Linux.

**How do I cancel, and can I get a refund?**
Cancel from **Help → Manage Subscription**. We offer a 14-day money-back guarantee -
see [Activation](activation.md) and [Support](support.md).

**Where are my settings and logs stored?**
Locally on your machine - see [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
