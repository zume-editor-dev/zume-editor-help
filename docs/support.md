# Support

- **Email:** [support@smolt-stage.com](mailto:support@smolt-stage.com)
- **Issues / questions:**
  [GitHub issues](https://github.com/zume-editor-dev/zume-editor-help/issues)

When contacting support, please include your operating system, the app version
(**Help → About**), and - for license questions - the email you purchased with.
Please do **not** share your full license key in a public issue.

For billing, cancellation and refunds, see [Activation & Licensing](activation.md).
