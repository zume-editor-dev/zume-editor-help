# Activation & Licensing

Zume Editor is a paid app with a free trial. You buy a subscription on our
website (all platforms), and activate the app with the license key we email you.

## Free trial

The first launch starts a **30-day free trial** with all features unlocked. No
account or payment is needed. When the trial ends, buy a license to keep using
the editor.

## Buying a license

Purchase on the website for **$49 / year** (Windows, macOS and Linux all use the
website). Payments are handled by **Paddle**, our reseller / Merchant of Record.
After payment you receive a **license key** by email (format
`ZUME-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX`).

## Activating

1. Open Zume Editor.
2. **Help → Activate License**.
3. Enter the **email** you purchased with and the **license key**.
4. Click **Activate**.

The app verifies your license online, then keeps working offline; it re-checks
quietly in the background from time to time.

## One license, up to 3 computers

A single license works on **up to 3 of your own computers** (any mix of Windows,
macOS and Linux), for one named user. Commercial use is allowed.

### Moving to another computer

If you reach the 3-device limit or replace a machine:

1. On the old computer: **Help → Deactivate This Device**.
2. On the new computer: **Help → Activate License** with the same email and key.

## Managing or cancelling your subscription

**Help → Manage Subscription** opens your secure Paddle portal page, where you can
update payment details or cancel. Cancelling stops future renewals; you keep
access until the end of the period you already paid for.

## Refunds

We offer a **14-day money-back guarantee**. If you are not satisfied, email
[support@smolt-stage.com](mailto:support@smolt-stage.com) within 14 days of
purchase for a full refund. See [Support](support.md).

## When a subscription ends

When a subscription is cancelled or a renewal fails, the app keeps working for a
short grace period, then prompts you to activate again. Your files are never
locked or altered - only the editor's licensed features stop until you renew.
