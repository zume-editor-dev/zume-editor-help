# Privacy & Security

- **No telemetry by default.** Zume Editor does not send your files or usage data
  anywhere unless you explicitly opt in.
- **Your data stays local.** Files, settings, recent lists and sessions are stored
  on your own machine.
- **Credentials are protected.** SSH, SFTP/FTP, database and cloud passwords are
  kept in your operating system's secure credential store (Windows Credential
  Manager, macOS Keychain, Linux Secret Service) - never in plain settings files.
- **License checks** contact only our activation server, and only to validate your
  license. See [Activation & Licensing](activation.md).
